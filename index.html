About

Hi, I'm Joashua Marl Barimbao — a BS Computer Science student in my third year, learning by building rather than just studying.

I've always been drawn to software development because there's always something new to pick up, and I try to stay hands-on with it. Dart and Flutter are where I've built my strongest footing — mobile development is my craft, and it's the space I keep sharpening with every project I take on.

I've also moved beyond just using AI — I work with AI-driven workflows, pipelines, and tools that enhance how I build and ship software. I believe that if you get left behind, it's hard to catch up — so I'd rather keep adapting than fall behind.

Being able to work with real clients and take on projects big enough to help support me as a student has taught me as much about humility as it has about code. Every opportunity that's come my way, I don't take for granted — it's by the grace of God that I've made it this far, and that keeps me grounded no matter what comes next.
